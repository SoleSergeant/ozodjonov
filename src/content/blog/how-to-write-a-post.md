This is a sample post. Because **Draft** is ticked, it never appears on the live site. Delete it once you've written your first real post.

## Making a new post

Open the admin at `/keystatic`, go to **Blog posts**, and click **Add**. Fill in the title, description, and date on the right, then write here. Untick **Draft** when it's ready and click **Save**.

## Formatting

Use the toolbar for headings, bold, links, and lists. Start a line with `>` for a big serif pull quote:

> A quote looks like this.

## Writing a movie reflection

Write a normal post, then open **Film diary**, find the film, and pick your post under **Reflection**. Its poster gets a "Read reflection" link.
