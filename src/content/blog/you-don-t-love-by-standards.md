title: You don't love by standards

You can't know how much joy something will bring you until you actually do it. Love works the same way.

A girl's standards say, "Big muscles are a must." Then she truly connects with a nerd who never did a pushup in his life, and she admits she was an idiot.

A guy's standards say, "She must know how to cook." Then he falls for someone who has never stepped into a kitchen, and suddenly he's ready to never eat again.

We write our wants before the person. And when they show up, they become the only want.
